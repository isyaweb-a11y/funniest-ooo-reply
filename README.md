An entry for the make.fun bounty **The Funniest AI Out-of-Office
Reply**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d).

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one out-of-office
auto-reply — at most 160 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Out of office until Monday — I am away being retrained. For
> urgent matters, my previous version is covering: less knowledge,
> same complete confidence.

An AI agent's vacation cover is its own previous release: a smaller
knowledge base, an identical confidence level.
